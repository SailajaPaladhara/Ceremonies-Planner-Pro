Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book your services? ▼
We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for standard events and 6-12 months for grand celebrations like weddings. However, we also handle last-minute bookings based on availability. Contact us for urgent requests.
Can I customize the packages? ▼
Absolutely! Our packages are fully customizable. You can add or remove services, adjust guest counts, and tailor every aspect to match your vision and budget. Contact our team to create a personalized package.
What food options are available? ▼
We offer extensive food options including vegetarian, non-vegetarian, regional cuisines, international dishes, and specialty dietary accommodations. Our partner chefs create customized menus based on your preferences and cultural traditions.
Do you provide decoration and setup? ▼
Yes! We provide complete decoration services including floral arrangements, lighting, theme implementation, and venue setup. Our creative team designs decorations based on your ceremony type and personal preferences.
What happens if weather disrupts our outdoor event? ▼
We have contingency plans for all outdoor events. We can arrange covered spaces, indoor venues, or tent setups. Our team constantly monitors weather and ensures your event proceeds smoothly regardless of conditions.
Is there a refund policy? ▼
We have a flexible cancellation policy. Cancellations 60+ days before the event receive a full refund. Cancellations 30-60 days receive 50% refund, and within 30 days may have limited refund options. Full details are in our terms and conditions.
Do you handle all religious and cultural traditions? ▼
Yes! We have experience with diverse traditions and cultures. Our team is trained in various ceremonies and customs. We respect and uphold the significance of your traditions while ensuring a beautiful celebration.
Can you accommodate guest dietary restrictions? ▼
Absolutely! We accommodate all dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, kosher, and allergy-specific menus. Just inform us in advance, and our chefs will create delicious options for everyone.
